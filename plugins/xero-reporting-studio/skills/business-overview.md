# Business Overview

Use `get_organisation_info`, `get_cash_position`, `get_aged_receivables`, and `get_aged_payables`.

Create a responsive two-column dashboard with bank account cards; invoices owed with count, overdue amount and ageing bars; bills to pay with the same structure; actionable tasks; recent invoice payments; six-month cash in/out and difference; YTD net profit with income and expenses; and an account watchlist. Clearly label bank-feed statement balances as unavailable when the connector does not expose them.

Validate receivables and payables totals against ageing buckets, cash difference = cash in  cash out, and net profit = income  expenses.
