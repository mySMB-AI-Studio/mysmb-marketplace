## Before accepting

- Confirm the Xero organisation and review date.
- Verify the listed invoice IDs and remaining balances in Xero.
- Check currency, partial-payment and source-data exceptions.
- Confirm the population was retrieved completely.
- Accept, request changes or close through the job’s review decision.

The report is a point-in-time internal review. Start a fresh review if the underlying invoices have changed.
